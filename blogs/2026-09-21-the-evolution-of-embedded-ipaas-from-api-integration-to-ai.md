No B2B SaaS product operates in isolation anymore. It sits inside a customer's existing technology stack, next to their CRM, their finance tools, their internal AI agents, and it's expected to work with all of it. Interoperability has moved from a nice-to-have to a baseline requirement and embedded iPaaS is the category that's had to evolve fastest to keep up.
